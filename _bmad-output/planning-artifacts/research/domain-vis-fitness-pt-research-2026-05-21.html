Market Dynamics and Growth

Growth drivers (high confidence):

  • 52% of personal trainers already use AI for programming (2026 State of the PT Industry Report). Adoption past tipping point.
  • Demand for individualized training + nutrition + mental-wellness bundled into one product.
  • Post-pandemic hybrid PT model (in-person + remote check-ins) — drives trainer-side software demand.
  • Wearable saturation (Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura, Garmin) creates HRV/sleep/recovery data supply.
  • Auto-regulation (RIR, e1RM, velocity-based) entering mainstream consumer apps via Bevel, Train Fitness AI, JuggernautAI.

Growth barriers:

  • iOS-dominant adoption (51.99% of fitness-app revenue). Android UX often deprioritized — Vis is RN, neutral.
  • North America 39.82% of revenue; India + APAC underserved at premium tier (opportunity for Vis given Mumbai-anchored branches).
  • Consumer apps churn fast; trainer-side software is sticky B2B.
  • Privacy regimes (GDPR, India DPDPA, US state laws) raising biometric-data compliance bar.

Industry Trends and Evolution (2026)

  • AI from feature to default. Glofox shipped AI workout recommendations in 2025; Mindbody is integrating with smart equipment for real-time usage. Trainerize, Everfit, TrueCoach all shipped AI programming assistants in 2025–2026.
  • Auto-regulation goes mainstream. RIR/RPE-based progression, velocity-based training, and e1RM-driven load suggestion moved from elite-strength niche (JuggernautAI, RTS) to consumer apps (Bevel, Train, Motra).
  • Recovery-aware programming. HRV + sleep + soreness inputs alter same-day session prescriptions. Whoop/Oura/Garmin integrations now table-stakes for premium tier.
  • Computer-vision form check. Bevel, Train Fitness AI, Sency-powered apps offering rep counting + bar-path + depth analysis from phone camera.
  • Hybrid coaching dominant. Pure remote PT and pure in-person both declining as % share; in-person + remote check-ins is the growth segment — Vis is built for this from day one.
  • Vertical integration into equipment + wearables. Mindbody × smart equipment; Apple Fitness+ × Apple Watch. Software-only platforms must integrate harder.
  • Multi-stakeholder consolidation slow. Despite obvious benefit, very few platforms model trainer + client + staff + owner as first-class roles with role-scoped data. This is Vis's white space.

A6. Progressive Overload — the one metric Vis must own

Combine per-exercise into a single Progression Index: 
ProgressionIndex = w_e1rm × Δe1RM + w_vol × ΔVolume + w_quality × ΔRPE_efficiency
Where RPE_efficiency = same load × same reps achieved at lower RPE. Three knobs the trainer can weight per client goal (strength / hypertrophy / endurance).

This is the surfaced output of all per-set inputs. Powers the recovery-orb-style "you progressed today" feedback the prototype hints at.

Step 6 — Research Synthesis Completed 2026-05-21

Executive Summary

Vis is being designed at a structurally rare moment. The fitness-app market splits into three competing categories that none of the incumbents has unified: AI-coached lifting (Motra, Bevel, Fitbod, JuggernautAI), PT-SaaS (Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, Caliber, Future), and multi-location gym ops (Mariana Tek, Hapana, Mindbody, Glofox). Each grows 10–19% CAGR. Each owns one user well and dismisses the other two as out of scope. Mariana Tek's May 2026 launch of Appointments to add 1:1 PT into ops software is the clearest market signal yet that convergence is starting.

Vis bets on full convergence — client + trainer + staff + owner as first-class roles in one role-scoped, multi-branch product. Two capabilities are red across every cluster surveyed: (1) trainer↔client real-time in-person session collaboration, and (2) cinematic on-brand UX in a multi-stakeholder product. These are Vis's defensible moats; everything else is competitive parity that must be cleared.

Training-science direction is settled. The 2025 network meta-analysis ranks autoregulation (APRE 93%, RPE 67%) far above fixed-percentage prescription (13%) for maximal strength; Schoenfeld's 2024 dose-response update confirms volume's diminishing returns above ~20 sets/muscle/week; e1RM from sub-maximal sets (Epley/Brzycki, ±5–10%) is accurate enough to drive a Progression Index without ever loading 1RM tests. Vis's training engine should be built on RIR, RPE, e1RM, set count per muscle, and on-device HealthKit/Health Connect biometrics — not a more elaborate science layer.

Regulatory direction is also settled, and favorably. The spec's choice to keep biometric data on-device, read-only, never persisted server-side collapses ~80% of the GDPR + India DPDPA compliance surface. DPDPA is fully in force as of May 2026 with penalties up to ₹250 crore, so this single architectural choice is also the most material risk mitigation in the product.

The remaining open questions are product, not strategy: how to make in-person real-time session collab feel native; how to present a Progression Index without hiding it behind charts; how to keep cinematic UX consistent across three engineering stacks (RN client, RN trainer, Angular admin) while three different teams ship in parallel.

Top 10 Strategic Takeaways

  1. The white space is convergence, not a better lifter. Don't let scope creep dilute the four roles.
  2. Real-time in-person session collab is the unique moat. WebSocket + polling fallback, last-write-wins per set row.
  3. Auto-regulation is now table stakes. RPE + RIR per set are first-class inputs from v1.
  4. Progression Index is the single derived metric to lead with. Three weights (e1RM, volume, RPE-efficiency), trainer-tunable per goal.
  5. Recovery orb stays. HRV + sleep + readiness shade trainer's intensity recommendation; auto-override is v2.
  6. "Biometric data stays on-device" is the most valuable line in the spec. Lock non-negotiable v1.
  7. AI = Claude + prompt caching, backend-proxied. ~90% cost cut. Outputs strongly typed.
  8. Trainer adoption is the riskiest beach. Win the gym institution; trainers come bundled.
  9. Defer EU to v2. India + global non-EU first.
  10. Visually-dated category is an opportunity. Prototype orb language is a real differentiator.

Architecture Implications

  • Real-time: Spring Boot WebSocket + RN (Socket.IO). Polling fallback. Last-write-wins per set row.
  • Biometric data: never persisted server-side v1.
  • AI proxy: Spring controller → Claude with system-prompt caching. Per-user TTL. Audit logs. Strongly-typed outputs.
  • Multi-branch: branch_id on every core entity; service-layer enforcement; OWNER unscoped.
  • Region: Cloud Run asia-south1 primary; asia-southeast1 backup.
  • DB: Neon PG. Flyway. set_logs with version column for optimistic locking.
  • Auth: Firebase Auth → FirebaseAuthFilter → UserService resolves role + branch_id.
  • Wearables: v1 = HealthKit + Health Connect read-only. v2 = Whoop / Oura / Garmin OAuth.
  • Observability: structured JSON logs; MDC = branch_id + role + user_id. OTel traces on AI calls.
